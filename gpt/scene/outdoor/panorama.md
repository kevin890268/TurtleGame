# Outdoor pond — panorama（四周遠景）

> 產出一張 **1536 × 1024 橫圖**，存成 `reference/scene/outdoor/panorama.png`。
> 這張會在池子四周繞一圈、**重複 6 次**，所以左右兩端要能接起來，而且不能有獨一無二的地標。

```
Create ONE wide landscape background image, 1536 x 1024 pixels.

PURPOSE:
This is the distant scenery that surrounds a small countryside pond in a cozy
turtle-raising game. It is wrapped around the scene like a panorama and
REPEATED 6 TIMES side by side. Therefore:
- The LEFT edge and the RIGHT edge must continue into each other seamlessly
  (same horizon height, same hill line, same colors at both edges).
- There must be NO unique landmark: no buildings, no houses, no temples,
  no roads, no bridges, no single tall tree, no mountain peak that stands out.
  Everything is a gentle, even, repeating countryside rhythm.

SCENE (low-elevation countryside in Taiwan, seen from the edge of a field):
- Bottom 12 percent: the near field. Soft, even green grass, the same green
  as a meadow (#6b9a45), almost no detail, so it blends into the 3D ground.
- Just above it: a band of rice paddies and low field edges, then soft clumps
  of bamboo and small broadleaf trees along the far field boundary.
- Behind that: low rolling green hills.
- Farthest: pale blue-grey mountains, hazy, very low contrast.
- The horizon (where the land meets the far hills) is LOW, around 35 percent
  up from the bottom of the image. The upper 60 percent is sky.
- Sky: soft daytime sky, light blue at the top fading to a warm pale cream
  near the horizon, with a few big soft rounded clouds.

ATMOSPHERE:
- Use atmospheric perspective: the farther a layer is, the lighter, bluer
  and lower in contrast it becomes.
- Calm, sunny, peaceful mid-morning light. No strong shadows.

STYLE:
- Japanese children's picture-book illustration.
- Soft hand-painted watercolor / gouache, gentle brush texture,
  warm earthy colors, muted olive greens, soft blues, cream.
- LOWER saturation and LOWER contrast than a character illustration:
  this is a background; a small turtle character will be placed in front of it
  and must stand out.
- Painterly and simple. No photorealism. No 3D render.

DO NOT DRAW:
- animals, birds, turtles, people
- buildings, fences, power lines, roads, vehicles
- text, labels, signatures, watermarks, borders, frames
- a sun disk (lighting only)

OUTPUT:
One single image, 1536 x 1024, full bleed, no margins.
```
