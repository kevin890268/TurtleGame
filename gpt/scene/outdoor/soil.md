# Outdoor pond — soil（土層剖面）

> 產出一張 **1024 × 1024 方圖**，存成 `reference/scene/outdoor/soil.png`。
> 這張會在池子正面的剖面上一直重複貼（上下左右都會接），所以要均勻、沒有特別顯眼的東西。

```
Create ONE square texture image, 1024 x 1024 pixels.

PURPOSE:
A seamless, tileable texture of an EARTH CROSS-SECTION, as if the ground
next to a countryside pond were cut straight down with a knife and we look
at the cut face from the side. It is repeated many times horizontally and
vertically on the front face of a cozy game diorama.
- It must tile seamlessly in BOTH directions: the left edge continues into
  the right edge, and the top edge continues into the bottom edge.
- Everything is evenly distributed. No single object stands out.

CONTENT:
- Horizontal soil layers (strata) in warm browns: some slightly lighter,
  some slightly darker, gently wavy boundaries between layers.
- Many small rounded pebbles embedded in the soil, in muted beige, grey and
  ochre, small and scattered evenly.
- A few very thin, fine roots running mostly downward.
- Subtle hand-painted grain.

STYLE:
- Japanese children's picture-book illustration.
- Soft hand-painted watercolor / gouache, warm earthy browns and ochres.
- Medium-LOW contrast: it is a background surface behind the game.
- Painterly and simple. No photorealism. No 3D render.

DO NOT DRAW:
- grass or plants on top, sky, water, the ground surface
- animals, worms, insects, bones, fossils, treasure, objects
- large rocks, one big root, or anything that would be noticed when repeated
- text, labels, borders, frames, shadows at the edges, vignette

OUTPUT:
One single image, 1024 x 1024, full bleed, flat even lighting.
```
