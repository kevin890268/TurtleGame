# Outdoor pond — grass（草地）

> 產出一張 **1024 × 1024 方圖**，存成 `reference/scene/outdoor/grass.png`。
> 這張會鋪滿池子四周的草地，一直重複貼，所以要均勻。

```
Create ONE square texture image, 1024 x 1024 pixels.

PURPOSE:
A seamless, tileable texture of a short meadow lawn seen from DIRECTLY ABOVE.
It covers the whole ground around a small countryside pond in a cozy game,
repeated many times.
- It must tile seamlessly in BOTH directions (left-right and top-bottom).
- Everything is evenly distributed. No single object stands out.

CONTENT:
- Short grass, main color soft meadow green (#6b9a45), with gentle variation:
  some slightly lighter yellow-green patches and slightly darker patches.
- Tiny grass blade strokes all over.
- Very sparse tiny clover leaves and a few tiny white or pale yellow wildflowers,
  small and evenly scattered.

STYLE:
- Japanese children's picture-book illustration.
- Soft hand-painted watercolor / gouache, gentle brush texture.
- LOW contrast, evenly lit from above, no cast shadows.
- Painterly and simple. No photorealism. No 3D render.

DO NOT DRAW:
- paths, stones, puddles, flower beds, bare soil patches
- animals, insects, turtles, people
- any perspective or horizon: the camera looks straight down
- text, labels, borders, frames, vignette

OUTPUT:
One single image, 1024 x 1024, full bleed, flat even lighting.
```
