CHARACTER DESIGN — CHINESE THREE-STRIPED BOX TURTLE

Create ONE consistent young Chinese three-striped box turtle
(Chinese: 斑龜, Mauremys reevesii).

This is the EXACT SAME turtle character that will be reused
across multiple animation sprite sheets.

CHARACTER IDENTITY:

The turtle must have a distinctive, consistent appearance.

Shell:
- moderately domed dark olive-brown shell
- slightly elongated oval shell
- shell is the dominant visual mass
- clearly visible individual scute plates
- natural dark brown scute boundaries
- subtle warm yellow-brown markings following the scute structure
- slightly darker outer shell edge
- matte painted surface
- no glossy plastic appearance

Head:
- relatively small head compared with the shell
- warm yellow-green skin
- slightly elongated turtle head
- rounded natural snout
- dark round eyes
- small natural eye highlight
- friendly but believable expression

Face and neck:
- recognizable Chinese three-striped box turtle markings
- three dark longitudinal stripes running along the head and neck
- stripes must remain clearly visible
- yellow-green base skin remains visible between the stripes
- markings should be symmetrical where anatomically appropriate

Body:
- four short sturdy turtle legs
- natural turtle feet
- clearly separated toes
- small natural tail
- moderately long neck
- realistic young-turtle proportions
- shell remains much larger than the head

PROPORTION:

Cute but believable.

NOT chibi.

NOT super-deformed.

NOT oversized head.

NOT oversized eyes.

The shell must remain the largest and most visually dominant
part of the character.

ART STYLE:

Japanese children's picture-book animal illustration.

Warm hand-drawn Japanese picture-book feeling.

Clean dark brown hand-drawn outlines.

Slightly organic and imperfect ink lines.

Soft opaque flat-color painting.

Subtle hand-painted texture.

Warm earthy palette:

- muted olive green
- dark olive brown
- warm brown
- yellow-green
- cream
- beige

Simple soft shading.

Minimal highlights.

Clear readable silhouette.

Carefully drawn animal anatomy.

The turtle should look like a professionally illustrated
Japanese children's picture-book character designed for
a cozy 2D turtle-raising game.

NOT watercolor painting.

NOT watercolor wash.

NOT watercolor bleeding.

NOT photorealistic.

NOT wildlife photography.

NOT 3D.

NOT CGI.

NOT anime.

NOT manga.

NOT cel shading.

NOT vector art.

NOT glossy.

NOT plastic.

NOT realistic skin texture.

NOT excessive scales.

NOT exaggerated cartoon proportions.

CHARACTER CONSISTENCY:

This exact turtle must remain identical in every future image.

Never redesign the shell.

Never change the shell color.

Never change the shell markings.

Never change the scute arrangement.

Never change the head shape.

Never change the eye shape.

Never change the facial stripes.

Never change the body proportions.

Never change the leg structure.

Never change the toe structure.

Never change the tail.

Only the POSE and VIEW ANGLE may change.

The turtle must always look like the same individual turtle.
