# ACTION 02 — WALK B
# 慢慢爬 / Slow Crawling

ACTION_ID:
02

ACTION_NAME:
walk_b

DESCRIPTION:
The turtle performs a slower, lower and more deliberate
forward crawling movement.

This is slower than WALK_A.

The turtle stays close to the ground with a slightly
lower body posture.

The movement must remain natural for a young
Chinese three-striped box turtle.

This is NOT running.
This is NOT a mammal crawl.
This is NOT belly-dragging.

==================================================
1. ANIMATION GOAL
==================================================

Create ONE continuous four-frame slow crawling cycle.

Frame sequence:

F1 → F2 → F3 → F4 → F1

The movement should be clearly slower and lower
than WALK_A.

==================================================
2. FRAME 1 — START
==================================================

The turtle begins in a low relaxed crawling posture.

Head is slightly extended forward.

Neck is relaxed.

The body is close to the ground.

Front legs are bent naturally.

Rear legs are positioned underneath the shell.

The shell remains rigid and stable.

==================================================
3. FRAME 2 — LOWER FORWARD STEP
==================================================

The turtle pushes forward gently.

One front leg reaches forward slightly.

The opposite rear leg provides a small push.

The head moves forward only a little.

The body advances close to the ground.

Movement is deliberate and slow.

==================================================
4. FRAME 3 — OPPOSITE STEP
==================================================

The opposite pair of legs takes the next step.

The turtle advances slightly farther.

The body remains low.

The shell stays stable.

The neck remains naturally extended.

The movement must remain subtle.

==================================================
5. FRAME 4 — RETURN
==================================================

The turtle completes the slow crawling cycle.

Leg positions transition naturally toward Frame 1.

The turtle remains low and relaxed.

Frame 4 must connect smoothly back to Frame 1.

==================================================
6. MOVEMENT RULES
==================================================

The primary movement comes from the legs.

The shell remains rigid.

The turtle does not bounce.

The turtle does not stretch or squash.

The belly may remain close to the ground,
but must not visibly drag unnaturally.

Do not make the turtle crawl like a mammal.

Do not make the turtle move like a lizard.

Do not create exaggerated leg movements.

==================================================
7. FOUR VIEW CONSISTENCY
==================================================

Apply the same physical crawling movement to:

F = Front 3/4
R = Right 3/4
B = Back 3/4
L = Left 3/4

All four views depict the same individual turtle.

==================================================
8. SPRITE SHEET
==================================================

Create exactly ONE 4 × 4 sprite sheet.

Columns:
Frame 1 | Frame 2 | Frame 3 | Frame 4

Rows:
F
R
B
L

The labels above are INTERNAL ORGANIZATION ONLY.
DO NOT DRAW THEM.

Exactly 16 cells.

Exactly ONE turtle per cell.

==================================================
9. BACKGROUND
==================================================

Pure solid chroma-key magenta.

RGB 255, 0, 255.

No environment.
No floor.
No water.
No props.
No particles.
No text.
No effects.

==================================================
10. FINAL REQUIREMENT
==================================================

The final sheet must show ONE EXACT SAME turtle
performing a slow, low and natural crawling cycle.

The turtle must remain visually identical to
character_reference.png and all rules in rules.md.
